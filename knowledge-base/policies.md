# Appointment & Clinic Policies

## 1. Booking Policy
- Appointments must be booked at least 2 hours in advance.
- When booking, patients must provide their full name and a valid phone number or email address.
- Booking confirmation will be sent via SMS/Email (simulated via n8n in this demo).

## 2. Cancellation Policy
- Patients can cancel their appointments at any time.
- We request that cancellations be made at least 12 hours prior to the scheduled time so the slot can be given to someone else.

## 3. Rescheduling Policy
- Rescheduling is subject to doctor availability.
- An appointment can be rescheduled without any extra fees.

## 4. Late Arrival Policy
- We grant a 15-minute grace period for appointments.
- If a patient arrives more than 15 minutes late, the appointment may be marked as canceled or moved to the end of the queue depending on the doctor's schedule.

## 5. Human Support Escalation
- If the AI Chatbot cannot resolve a patient's query or if the patient requests human assistance, the chatbot will capture the patient's contact details as a "support lead" and a human receptionist will call them back within 24 hours.
