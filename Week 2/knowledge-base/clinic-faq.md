# Synexus Demo Clinic - Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

## 1. What are the clinic's operating hours?
The clinic is open from **Monday to Saturday, 08:00 AM to 06:00 PM**. Please note that specific doctor timings vary (refer to the Doctors schedule). We are closed on Sundays and all public holidays.

## 2. Where is the clinic located?

## 4. Do I need an appointment, or can I walk in?
We highly recommend booking an appointment in advance to avoid long waiting times. However, walk-ins are accepted in person for general consultations and lab tests based on daily availability. Note: this chatbot only handles scheduled bookings — walk-in slots are managed on-site by clinic staff.

## 5. What payment methods are accepted?
We accept cash, debit/credit cards (Visa/Mastercard), and major mobile wallets (JazzCash, EasyPaisa).

## 6. How do I get my lab test results?
Lab results (such as CBC Blood Tests) are typically emailed to the patient within 24 hours of the test. You can also pick up a physical copy from the reception.

## 7. Are you open on public holidays?
No, the clinic is closed on Sundays and all public/religious holidays (e.g., Eid, national holidays). Please check with reception ahead of major holidays if you're unsure, or ask this chatbot for the current status.

## 8. Do you provide emergency services?
We are a standard outpatient clinic. We do not provide 24/7 emergency services. In case of a severe medical emergency, please visit the nearest hospital emergency room.
